## Deviations, with reasons

1. **Background cues are proven with a test harness, not on a device** (goal condition 2 allows this). expo-speech, expo-haptics and expo-audio are recording stand-ins in `jest.setup.js`; the manual test is above. iOS keeps the system voice alive through the audio session; on Android a backgrounded JS thread may be throttled even with the media service declared, and the runner then catches up (tested) — to be checked on a device.
2. **No audio files**: the cues are the device's text-to-speech voice (FR or EN) and the vibration motor. Nothing needs a sound licence (asset register AST-003). Three new MIT dependencies: expo-speech, expo-haptics, expo-audio (SBOM, `pnpm legal:licences` green).
3. **ENGINE_VERSION 0.3.0 → 0.4.0; SESSION_RULES_VERSION 0.2.0 → 0.3.0.** Program sessions with conditioning now carry a cardio block, a program's intervals now also need the two weeks of training, and the impact default applies to exercise choice. The server refuses 0.3.0 sessions. The goldens changed deliberately:
   - `P2`, `P3`, `P5` `.sessions.json`: only the engine version in their safety events (0.3.0 → 0.4.0).
   - `P1.sessions.json`: a new `cardio` projection line per session showing P1's 10-minute steady finisher as the device runs it (brisk walk 540 s moderate + 60 s easy; impact ≤ low because of the knee limitation and BMI ≈ 38; zones by perceived exertion). No set, load, reserve or exercise changed for any persona.
   - `packages/exercise-library/src/__fixtures__/session-personas.ts`: `heightCm` for P1 (178 cm) and P2 (165 cm), the two personas the vision document gives a height for; null for the others.
4. **"≥ 2 weeks of consistent training"** is read as: the first logged session is at least 14 days old, and each of the last two 7-day windows has at least 2 logged sessions (`hiit.minSessionsPerWeek`, engineering default). A session counts when a set was done or a cardio block was run. Open question 1.
5. **EMOM and AMRAP are not gated as HIIT**: they are prescribed at a moderate, controlled effort (RPE 4–6) and count as moderate minutes. A vigorous custom protocol is gated as HIIT. Open question 2.
6. **A standalone HIIT request that the gates refuse is refused, with its reason** (`cardio.unavailable.*`), not downgraded silently; the screen shows HIIT and Tabata as "not available yet" and offers steady cardio. A program's interval block becomes steady and says why (as M02 already did for S1).
7. **A "less ready" readiness check (M05) keeps HIIT for another day** (`cardio.unavailable.readiness_reduced`); other cardio stays moderate. Not in the spec; it follows M05's rule that readiness never makes a session harder.
8. **The impact default applies to the whole session**, not only to the cardio block: the spec says "Impact levels … per exercise; the default is set from SafetyProfile, bodyweight and joint flags". A knee/ankle/hip flag or BMI ≥ 35 lowers the impact ceiling of strength-slot choices, swaps and pain replacements too. On the seed personas this changed nothing (P1 already had a low ceiling from the knee limitation).
9. **Zone bands are engineering defaults**: light 30–39 %, moderate 40–59 %, vigorous 60–84 % of the heart-rate reserve; RPE 2–3 / 4–6 / 7–8. The spec names the method (Karvonen, Tanaka) but not the bands. Heart-rate zones need both a resting heart rate and a date of birth, and a reserve of at least 40 bpm (`hrr.minReserveBpm`); otherwise effort and the talk test only.
10. **Resting heart rate is kept in memory on the device** (`useManualHeartRate`): it is re-entered after an app restart. Persisting it, and reading it from a wearable, is M12's job through the `HeartRateSource` port. It travels to the server only inside the session input (a health collection). Live heart-rate readings are never logged or sent.
11. **Custom work/rest intervals are built by the engine but not offered on the screen yet** (five protocols on the screen: steady, HIIT, Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP). A picker for work, rest and rounds is UI work for M13/M14.
12. **Venue swaps happen when the session is built** (the requested movement, or its swap partner, or the place's preferred machine), not mid-block: the cardio block has no in-run "change movement" control. `cardioAlternatives` exists in the engine for it.
13. **A red pain rating (≥ 6) during a cardio block ends the block** and saves what was done. This is stricter than M02's in-session S2 (which swaps exercises), because a cardio block's movement loads the same joints all the way through.
14. **The server bounds `cardio_done` by its stored block** (same plan, same protocol, no more moderate or vigorous seconds than planned, the same number of work steps). It cannot check the seconds against real time.
15. **The server now checks the stored height and weight for every session** (`session.biometrics_mismatch`), because the impact default reads them. Sessions from devices that do not send them are compared as null with the stored values, so the existing M02/M05 tests (profiles without biometrics) are unchanged.
16. **Six new seed movements and two new equipment ids** (elliptical, stair climber), because the spec's steady modalities and venue swaps need them. They carry the same review status as every seed entry (`validated: false`, A2/A3 pending) and FR/EN wording; the equipment preset `full_gym` includes the two machines.
17. **No Maestro flow** for M03: the goal does not ask for one; the journeys are proven in jest-expo.
18. **Existing tests changed; no assertion removed or loosened:**
    - `packages/engine/src/config/config.test.ts`: the list of engine configs gains `cardio`; its values pass the same checks (source, `validated: false`).
    - `packages/engine/src/session/program-session.test.ts` (M02): "cleared: intervals stay intervals" now also gives two weeks of logged training (the M03 rule). The original assertion is unchanged; a new assertion shows that without the training the intervals become steady with `cardio.hiit.needs_consistent_training`.
    - `packages/exercise-library/src/session.test.ts`: the golden projection gains the `cardio` lines and passes `heightCm`; goldens regenerated as in deviation 3.
    - `tooling/legal/test/legal-tooling.test.ts`: the exact asset-register count 5 → 6 (AST-003), with a comment, as M06 did for 4 → 5; a new M03 claims test.
    - `packages/legal/src/release-defensibility-claims.test.ts`: new M03 claims cases (added, none changed).
    - `apps/mobile/__tests__/app-config.test.ts`: a new test for the expo-audio plugin settings.
    - `apps/mobile/jest.setup.js`: stand-ins for expo-speech, expo-haptics and expo-audio (no native modules under jest).
19. **No device, no Docker, CI not run on GitHub.** Integration tests run on local PostgreSQL 16 and Redis.
