## Deviations from the spec, with reasons

1. **Goals and experience share one screen.** Counting the M17 age gate as the first screen of a fresh install, a separate experience screen would make 13. Both are short single choices.
2. **Health-data consent comes before "About you".** Weight and body fat are health data (GDPR Art. 9); consent is asked before any health data is entered. The spec's order of items (goals, experience, schedule, equipment, biometrics, screening) is kept.
3. **No PAR-Q+.** Its licence is unchecked (RSK-08). Ten original questions cover similar ground in the app's own words; the outcomes are the spec's (cleared / cleared with restrictions / consult a professional). A1 and B3 decide on a licensed instrument.
4. **Date of birth is asked again in "About you".** The M17 gate promises the date "is not kept" (and its test checks it). The profile stores it, with its own explanation, for age-based rules (S4 under 18, S7) and the new server-side age check; it goes through the same S7 gate (a block locks the app).
5. **Target weight is not collected** (deferred to M10, where the S4 floor "no goal weight below BMI 18.5" is enforced). Body fat is optional and deferrable.
6. **"Exercises to avoid"**: the profile field and `SafetyProfile.excludedExerciseIds` exist and filter the pool, but there is no picker yet (natural home: the library screen). Limitations are the seven M06 joints (no neck), without free text.
7. **Dynamic profile fields** (bodyweight trend M04, training age, capacity estimates M07) are not built: they need M04/M07 data. (Capacity estimates: built by M07 as the CapacityModel, see docs/status/M07.md.) Reminder preference is stored; notifications are M13.
8. **Consult-a-professional users can still reach the first workout**, at S1-capped intensity (RPE ≤ 7, no HIIT, no maximal tests), as S1 allows. Pregnancy/postpartum users see professional guidance and only the low-intensity library.
9. **The first-workout screen is a gate and a place picker**, not a session: prescriptions come from the engine (M02, not built). It shows the L3 notice, the place and its exercise pool.
10. **The welcome screen stays the landing screen** after the age gate (M17/M06 tests expect it); it offers "Set up your training" instead of an automatic redirect.
11. **Consents on a second device** are not pulled (the API returns states, not records): the selector on a new device is `not_screened` until the user consents there. Open question.
12. **After a health-consent withdrawal the device ignores its screening records** (the selector fails closed) and erases the draft answers and measurements, but the append-only local screening records remain until the account wipe; the server erases its copies at once.
13. **Device time of offline records** is accepted within 30 days (config); older offline records are refused by the server and stay in the device ledger only.
14. ~~**Safety events for a stored screening are written after the sync transaction** (ADR-013 consequences).~~ **Fixed in `3bff4b8`** (found in PO review, fixed during the M07 run): the sync listener now runs inside the sync transaction and writes the events with it; a failed log write rolls back the screening and fails the push, and the device retries. Test: `apps/api/test/integration/profile.test.ts` › `stores a screening and its safety events atomically …` (failure injected by a trigger on `defensibility_events`).
15. **No device**: Maestro flows written, not run; journeys proven in jest-expo over sql.js. **Docker unavailable**: integration tests on local PostgreSQL 16 and Redis. **CI not executed on GitHub** (not pushed).
16. **Existing tests changed, no assertion removed or loosened:**
    - `packages/shared/src/exercise.test.ts`: the SafetyProfile schema now requires the M01 fields; the "valid" example gained them and a new assertion shows the M06-only object is now rejected.
    - `packages/engine/src/substitution.test.ts`, `packages/exercise-library/src/substitute.test.ts`: SafetyProfile fixtures/arbitraries gained the M01 fields with their unrestricted values (the substitution filters do not read them). `UNRESTRICTED_SAFETY_PROFILE` gained them too.
    - `apps/mobile/__tests__/boot.test.tsx`, `age-gate.e2e.test.tsx`, `library.test.tsx`: their route maps gained the M01 routes (`...m01Routes`), as M06 did for `library`, to avoid expo-router's missing-route warning.
