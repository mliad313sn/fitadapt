## Deviations from the spec, with reasons

1. **The Maestro flow is not executed** (no device). The journey is proven in jest-expo through the real router and on-device SQLite, offline, and the flow's ids are checked against the app.
2. **No multi-device client in the app yet.** The relay (REST + WebSocket), its protocol contracts and the pure projection are built and proven with two real socket clients in the integration suite; the app's single-device mode is complete. A device screen for "join with a code" is the next step (M13/M19), on the same `PairClientMessage` protocol.
3. **Local-network fallback, the remote "ghost" session and group mode (v2)** are not built. The spec lists them in scope; the goal conditions do not require them. The engine's `mergePlans` and `fairScore` work on a recorded partner plan, which is what a ghost session needs.
4. **The pair session is shorter per person than a solo session in the same time.** Turns take longer, so both plans are generated for fewer minutes until the timeline fits the smaller of the two budgets (never below 15 minutes each; then the timeline says it runs over). On the seed, P1 and P2 each get 24 of 40 minutes and the pair session takes 39.9 minutes.
5. **"Or swap" for an equipment conflict** is the person's own M02 swap or skip; the planner staggers turns on a single implement and adds a changeover only when both load it.
6. **Conditioning in a pair session** is a "together" step (Done/Skip) on the pair screen: the M03 eyes-free interval runner is not embedded there, so no `cardio_done` minutes are logged from a pair session. The prescription is unchanged.
7. **The swap sheet and the post-session and next-morning pain checks** of the solo workout screen are not on the pair screen; every set can be skipped and each person can stop (L4), and the in-session pain flag (S2) and red flag (S3) are there for each person.
8. **A guest has no M07 assessment on the owner's phone**: their first pair session starts at the lowest rung of each ladder (the engine's rule for untested slots) and progresses from their own logs. A guest can run the assessment later with their own account.
9. **The owner's account wipe also erases the guests' data on the phone** (`wipeLocalDatabase`, unchanged): it is on the owner's device, and keeping another person's health data after a reset would be worse. The consent text and the records of processing say the data stays on this phone.
10. **Engine version not bumped** (see the header): no individual prescription rule changed.
11. **Existing tests changed; no assertion removed or loosened:**
    - `packages/shared/src/privacy.test.ts`: the exact list of consent data types gains `partner_sharing`.
    - `packages/engine/src/config/config.test.ts`: the exact list of engine configs gains `pair` (its values are checked by the same assertions).
    - `packages/legal/src/legal.test.ts`: the exact list of notice triggers gains `pair.challenge.start`.
    - `apps/api/test/integration/auth.test.ts`: the exact OpenAPI path list gains `/v1/pair/sessions` and `/v1/pair/sessions/join`.
    - `apps/api/test/integration/privacy.test.ts`: the exact list of consent states gains `partner_sharing`; the inventory test's user also starts a pair session so that every inventory section, including the new pair ones, is present (the assertion is unchanged).
    - `apps/api/test/integration/harness.ts`: `TRUNCATE` also clears the three pair tables.
    - `apps/mobile/__tests__/routes.tsx` (route map, no assertions): the `pair` route.
12. **Lockfile incident, root-caused.** A first `pnpm add ws@8.18.3` made pnpm re-point jsdom's and `@expo/ws-tunnel`'s `ws` to 8.18.3, which `pnpm security:audit` flags high (GHSA memory exhaustion, fixed in 8.21.0). Fixed in `bfd7b09` by re-adding `ws@8.21.3` on the pre-M09 lockfile: every consumer now gets 8.21.3; the audit is back to `critical 0, high 0, moderate 3`.
13. **No device, no Docker, CI not run on GitHub.** Integration tests on local PostgreSQL 16 and Redis.

## Open questions

1. Should the owner's account reset keep a guest's data on the phone (current: erased with the reset)? (B1, PO)
2. How long should the multi-device relay keep a session's events once it is over (current: account lifetime, erased on a partner_sharing withdrawal)? A short period (days) seems right. (B1)
3. Should a partner who stops early still see their own score (current: the score is shown only when both completed)? (A6)
4. Should the pair session keep each person's full solo minutes and run longer, instead of shortening both to the time both have? (A3, PO)
5. Should a guest be offered the M07 assessment on the owner's phone, or only with their own account? (A3, B1)
6. Is "the same share of each own prescription" the right operational meaning of "equal relative effort", or should RIR reported per set enter it? (A5)
