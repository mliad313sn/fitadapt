## Deviations from the spec, with reasons

1. **No calorie number for any deficit-disabled user**, whatever the goal (a 16–17-year-old asking for muscle gain gets habits, not a surplus target). S4 only requires deficit features off; showing numbers to minors and to users advised against restriction is a disordered-eating risk. Stricter default; open question for A4.
2. **Deficit features are also off when no valid screening exists and for S7 pregnancy/postpartum** (fail closed). M01's SafetyProfile sets `deficitNutritionAllowed: false` for "not screened" but not for pregnancy; the S4 check adds it without changing the M01 profile.
3. **The M04 hand-off pauses the deficit (14 days), then caps the pace at 0.5 %/week (to day 56)** rather than only "reducing" it: M04's notice already promises "will not suggest eating less for now". Both numbers are engineering defaults, validated:false.
4. **No third-party food database.** An original seed of 270 estimated items instead (licence risk RSK-08; share-alike refused). "Community submissions verified before publication" (spec Scope) is not built (needs M13 moderation and A4 review).
5. **Barcode scan (v1.1) and photo-assisted estimates (v2)** are not built, as planned by the spec.
6. **The sex term of the equation is asked in the nutrition set-up** (M01 does not collect sex); "prefer not to say" uses the midpoint constant (−78). Height is asked there too when the profile has none (kept in the nutrition settings); weight is saved as an M04 weigh-in.
7. **Supportive mode hides the quick log and search as well** (number-based tracking disabled, spec Rules); habits stay.
8. **`ENGINE_VERSION` stays 0.4.0** (ADR-022): no training prescription changed and the goldens are untouched; nutrition targets carry `NUTRITION_RULES_VERSION` 0.1.0.
9. **`/nutrition` is behind the workout L2 gate** (onboarding, Terms, Privacy, health consent, exercise risk), so a SafetyProfile always exists before any nutrition number.
10. **Local food names are outside the FR/EN catalogues** (`foodLocalName` in packages/i18n): they are the same in both languages, and the existing parity test requires FR and EN messages to differ. Some English food names were made more descriptive for the same reason (e.g. "Butter croissant", "Kiwi fruit").
11. **Plans are ordered by a `supersedes` chain** besides their time (the sync client orders records by time then id, so two plans in the same millisecond were misordered in fake-timer tests).
12. **No KPI events** (weekly logging days, protein attainment, guardrail trigger rate): the analytics pipeline is M18.
13. **No device, no Docker, CI not run on GitHub**: jest-expo journeys, local PostgreSQL 16 and Redis; the Maestro flow is written, not executed.
14. **Existing tests changed; no assertion removed or loosened:**
    - `packages/engine/src/config/config.test.ts`: the exact list of `ENGINE_CONFIGS` keys gains `nutrition` (every value still checked for source and validated:false).
    - `packages/sync/src/test/sync.test.ts`: three new append-only collections asserted (additions only).
    - `packages/privacy/src/consent.test.ts`: the exact list of features the health consent enables gains `nutrition.tracking`.
    - `tooling/legal/test/legal-tooling.test.ts`: the exact register count 6 → 7 (DATA-004).
    - `apps/mobile/__tests__/routes.tsx`: the route map gains `nutrition` (as earlier modules did).
