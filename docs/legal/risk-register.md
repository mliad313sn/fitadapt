# Legal risk register

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** Starting point from `docs/specs/00-legal-framework.md`. Exposure and controls are engineering descriptions, not legal assessments. "M20 control in code" names what exists in this repository today; everything else is planned in the named modules. Status is `open` for every risk: a risk is only accepted or closed by the Product Owner with counsel.

| Domain | Exposure | Controls | Modules | M20 control in code | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal injury / negligence | A user is injured following a prescription or during a partner challenge. | S1–S7, L3, L4, conservative defaults, expert-validated content, L11 audit trail, insurance, terms with lawful limitation clauses. | M01–M05, M09, M20 | Exercise-risk acknowledgment and first-workout gate (`firstWorkoutGate`); point-of-risk notices; hash-chained defensibility log with safety events and engine versions; legal-hold export. M09: each partner accepts their own texts and consents; each partner's own SafetyProfile, pain flags and S3 lock in the shared session; Fair Challenge off by default, scored only against each person's own plan, nothing counted after pain, with its own point-of-risk notice (`pair_challenge`) per partner; pair events in each person's own chain. | open |
| Medical-device regulation | Features or copy imply a medical purpose. | L1 claims linter, no diagnosis, no condition-specific programs, regulatory review of screening, pain and nutrition copy. | M05, M10, M11, M20 | `pnpm legal:claims` over catalogues, store metadata, prompts and eval outputs (FR/EN). | open |
| Regulated professions | Individual nutrition or rehabilitation advice treated as unlicensed practice. | General guidance only, referral to professionals, credential checks for coaches. | M05, M10, M15 | Nutrition-deficit and AI notices say "general guidance, not a prescription". | open |
| Data protection | Unlawful processing of health data, breach, missing filings or unlawful transfers. | M17 + L9, DPIA, consent records, encryption, filings, transfer mechanisms, breach runbook. | M17, M12, M04 | Consent texts per data type (drafts) linked to M17 consent versions; authority filings tracked per market (all open). | open |
