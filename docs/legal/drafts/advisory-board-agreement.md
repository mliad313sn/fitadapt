# Advisory-board agreement — draft v1 / Convention de conseil scientifique — projet v1

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** PROJET — revue par un avocat requise. Structure for counsel (Gate 0, "Advisory board"). Not legal advice. The committee personas in the specifications are fictional; this agreement is for **real** experts and none has been signed. No real person's name may be used publicly without the written consent this agreement records (L12).

3. **Independence and limits.** Advice is professional review of general content, not care for any individual user. The advisor does not see identifiable user data unless a separate data agreement is signed.
4. **Compensation.** [Fee, equity or unpaid, to be agreed.]
5. **Liability.** [Allocation and limits, and whether the company's insurance covers the advisor, to be drafted by counsel.]
6. **Confidentiality.** [Terms to be drafted.]

8. **Name and likeness.** The company may / may not [choose] use the advisor's name, title and photo publicly, only as approved in writing, and must stop on withdrawal.
9. **Term and termination.** [To be set.]
