# Gate 0 — founder protections checklist

> **DRAFT — requires counsel review.** Source: `docs/specs/00-legal-framework.md`, Gate 0, and decision C12. This is a list of actions **a human (the founder, with counsel) must take**; software cannot do them. Every item starts `open`. An item may be closed only by the founder, with evidence (a signed document, a filing receipt, a counsel memo) stored outside this repository and referenced here, in a reviewed change. No item has been started by the M20 engineering work.

| Item | Action | When | Owner | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company structure | Incorporate a limited-liability entity before any public release; the company (not the founder personally) contracts with users, coaches and suppliers and owns all code, content and trademarks. | Before beta | Founder with corporate counsel | open | — |
| IP ownership | Written IP assignment from every contributor (co-founders, freelancers, advisors, content creators) to the company; keep a register. | Continuous | Founder | open | — |
| Founder employment check | If the founder is employed, review the employment contract for IP-assignment, confidentiality, outside-activity and non-compete clauses; build on personal time and equipment; obtain written approval where required. | Now | Founder with employment counsel | open | — |
| Counsel | Engage counsel in the primary jurisdiction and correspondents for each launch market; agree the jurisdiction matrix and document list. | Phase 0 | Founder | open | — |
| Name clearance | Trademark search (e.g. Nice classes 9, 41, 42, 44) in launch markets; file only after clearance; keep two fallback names. See trademark-register.md. | Phase 0 | Founder with trademark counsel | open | — |
| Insurance | Quotes for product/professional liability covering digital fitness guidance, cyber liability, and D&O once investors join. The defensibility file (defensibility-file.md) is the evidence insurers usually ask for. | Before beta | Founder with a broker | open | — |
| Advisory board | Written agreements with real experts: scope, compensation, liability, confidentiality, and whether their names may be used publicly (draft: drafts/advisory-board-agreement.md). Until signed, no real person's name is used (L12). | Phase 1 | Founder | open | — |
| Data-protection filings | Plan registrations or authorisations with authorities where required (e.g. prior formalities with Senegal's CDP under Law No. 2008-12) and appoint representatives where needed (e.g. GDPR Art. 27 if established outside the EU). Per-market items: jurisdiction-matrix.md. | Before beta | Founder with privacy counsel | open | — |
